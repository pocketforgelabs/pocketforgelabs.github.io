Rift Vision

Scan your cards. Know what you pulled.

A companion app for the Riftbound trading card game. Point the camera at a card: it is recognised on your phone, priced and added to your collection. Build decks, track trades, and export your pack openings as videos with prices.

Coming soon toGoogle PlayComing soon toApp Store

Android · iPhone

Features

Everything a collector needs.

Scan as you open

On-device card recognition reads the art, name and collector number. No typing, and it works offline.

Prices for every printing

Market prices for foils, alt arts, showcases and signatures, refreshed daily.

Your collection

Set completion, filters, values and where each card is stored, from binders to deck boxes.

Deck builder

Legend, champion, sideboard and the official rules, with what you are missing and what it costs.

Trades and stats

Scan both sides of a trade, and see whether each box or pack paid off.

Videos with prices

Record your opening and export it with each card and its price on screen.

Privacy

Your data stays yours.

Your collection, decks and videos stay on your phone. Card recognition runs on the device; camera frames never leave it. There are no ads, no analytics and no account to create: an optional Google sign-in is used only for backups to your own Drive and, on Android, the free trial.
