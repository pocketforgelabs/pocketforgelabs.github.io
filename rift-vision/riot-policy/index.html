Rift Vision

Riot Games policy

Rift Vision is a fan-made companion tool for the Riftbound trading card game, made by Pocket Forge LLC. It uses Riot Games’ card names, text and artwork so you can recognise, organise and price the cards you own. Riot Games owns all of that content. We do not.

Rift Vision was created under Riot Games' "Legal Jibber Jabber" policy using assets owned by Riot Games. Riot Games does not endorse or sponsor this project.

A tool, not a game

Rift Vision is not a game and does not let you play Riftbound. It helps with the physical cards you already own:

  • Scanning cards with the camera as you open packs
  • Keeping a collection, wishlist and trade list
  • Showing recent market prices
  • Building decks with the official deck-building rules
  • Browsing the card library

What Rift Vision does not do

  • No digital Riftbound gameplay and no rules enforcement during play
  • No matchmaking, ranks or ladders
  • No betting, gambling, loot boxes or random paid rewards
  • No blockchain, crypto or NFTs
  • No play rates, win rates or match-up data
  • No selling of Riot’s data to anyone
  • No Riot logos, and no suggestion that Riot made, endorses or sponsors the app

Riot’s content

Riftbound, League of Legends, and all card names, card text, artwork, set names and symbols are trademarks or copyrights of Riot Games, Inc. Rift Vision shows card text in Riot’s official English wording. Card content is used only to identify and display cards. It is not sold on its own.

The policies we follow

Rift Vision is built to follow:

  • Riot Games’ Legal Jibber Jabber policy
  • The Riftbound developer policy

Rift Vision is free to use. Rift Vision Pro adds extra features, and every card stays viewable in the free version.

For Riot Games and rights holders

If anything in Rift Vision or on this site should change, email dev@pocketforgelabs.com. We will reply quickly and make the change.
