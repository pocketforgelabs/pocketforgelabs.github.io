What the app is

Rift Vision is a companion app for the Riftbound trading card game: it recognises cards as you open packs, shows market prices, and keeps your collection and decks. It is a fan-made tool by Pocket Forge LLC.

Prices are informational

Prices shown are recent market prices from a third-party service, refreshed about once a day and provided as-is. They may be out of date or wrong, and they are not an offer to buy or sell. Do your own checking before any trade or sale.

Recognition is best effort

Card recognition can miss a card or identify the wrong printing. Review what was added, and use the app’s editing tools to correct it. The app is not a substitute for checking cards yourself.

Rift Vision Pro

The app is free to use, with a daily scan allowance. Rift Vision Pro unlocks the rest (unlimited scanning, Stats, video export with prices, trades, Google Drive backup and on-demand price refresh) for a one-time purchase through Google Play or the App Store. It is not a subscription and nothing renews.

You can try Pro free for 5 days, once. Nothing is charged for the trial; when it ends, the Pro features lock again until you buy Pro, and everything you scanned stays.
