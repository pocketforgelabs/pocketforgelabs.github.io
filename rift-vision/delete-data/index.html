Rift Vision

Delete your data

Rift Vision keeps no account of its own

Rift Vision does not create accounts and has no server of its own: your collection, decks, settings and videos are stored only on your phone.

Delete your account and data in the app

Open Rift Vision → Settings → Account → Delete account and data. This deletes your backup from your Google Drive, removes Rift Vision’s access to your Google account and signs you out. Turn on “Also erase this phone” to delete your collection, decks, history, settings and in-app recordings too.

Delete everything on your phone

Uninstall Rift Vision. This deletes its database and settings. Recorded videos stay in your gallery until you delete them there.

Delete your Google Drive backup

If you backed up to Google Drive, the backup is in a private app folder of your own Drive. Open drive.google.com → Settings → Manage apps → Rift Vision → Options → Delete hidden app data.

Remove Rift Vision’s access to your Google account

Sign out in the app (Settings → Account → Sign out), then remove Rift Vision at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app keeps no copy of your Google profile after you sign out.

Questions

Email dev@pocketforgelabs.com and we will help. We hold no personal data about you on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete, but we are happy to walk you through the steps.
